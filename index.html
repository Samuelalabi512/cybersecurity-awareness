About CyberSafe

CyberSafe Digital Solutions was established in 2026 with a vision of promoting cybersecurity awareness and helping individuals and organisations stay safe in the digital world. Our journey began in 2025 when we developed an interest in cybersecurity and started learning about online threats, phishing attacks, password security and digital protection. Inspired by the growing need for online safety, we created CyberSafe Digital Solutions as a platform for cybersecurity education, awareness and digital services. Our goal is to make cybersecurity knowledge easier to understand and accessible to schools, small businesses, NGOs and individuals.
